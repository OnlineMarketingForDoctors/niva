Contrast. The sky accent passes only on navy. The live site also uses it for text and button fills on white and light blue, where it fails WCAG AA. The rebuild should keep sky for icons and on-navy text and use the blue where sky text or fills sit on light backgrounds.

Section patterns

Backgrounds alternate light blue → white → navy. Each section opens with an optional sky eyebrow, a 42px heading and a 17px intro, 20px apart. The hero uses the 60px display size and curves its bottom corners at 33px.
