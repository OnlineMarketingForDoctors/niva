Questions people ask before they book

Do I have to leave my NHS GP?

No.

Niva works alongside your NHS care, not instead of it. You stay registered exactly where you are and use us whenever you want faster access, a longer appointment or a second opinion.

Do I have to leave my NHS GP?

No. Niva complements NHS care rather than replacing it. You stay registered with your NHS GP and use Niva whenever you want faster access, a longer appointment, a second opinion or preventive care. Where it helps, we write to your NHS GP so your records stay joined up.

Will my insurance cover it?

It depends on your policy. Many international policies, and UK policies with an outpatient or cash benefit, allow you to claim some or all of the cost back. Check your policy terms before booking. We provide receipts and any documentation your insurer asks for.

How much is it, and are there hidden costs?

A 20-minute appointment is £80 face-to-face, or £70 by video or telephone. A 30-minute appointment is £110 face-to-face or £90 by telephone or video. Referrals and private prescriptions are included at no extra charge. Weekend and bank holiday appointments carry a £10 supplement. There are no other charges for a GP appointment. Any tests are priced separately and agreed with you first.

Can I be seen today?

Usually, yes. We offer same-day face-to-face, telephone and video appointments, subject to availability, including weekday evenings and Saturdays. Book online to see today's slots, or call the clinic and reception will find the earliest time.

Can a Private GP refer me?

Yes. We arrange private specialist referrals and, where appropriate, write to your NHS GP or make recommendations for NHS referral. The exact NHS route depends on local referral criteria. For certain hospitals we can make direct urgent suspected cancer referrals into NHS pathways where the criteria are met.

Do you do scans?

Blood tests and 12-lead ECGs are done at the clinic. We do not perform ultrasound, X-ray, CT or MRI on site. When imaging is needed our GPs refer you to trusted partner providers, then review the results with you and plan the next steps.

Are you a walk-in clinic?

We are an appointment-based clinic. We strive to provide you with an appointment on the same day or within 24 to 48 hours. If you have urgent needs and cannot secure an appointment online, please contact us and we will do our best to accommodate you.
