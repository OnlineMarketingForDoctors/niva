About Us

Experience Holistic GP Services Tailored To Your Needs

At Niva Medical Clinic, we provide a comprehensive Private GP service in Stanmore that goes beyond standard practice. Our dedicated team offers high-quality healthcare services, including Private GP consultations, Physiotherapy, and Psychological support, all within a modern, purpose-built clinic on Stanmore Broadway. We aim to complement NHS care by enhancing accessibility, providing longer appointment times, ensuring continuity in care, and emphasising preventive healthcare.

Our mission is to empower you to take control of your health while restoring the traditional "family doctor" model of ongoing, personalised care. We focus on building long-lasting relationships with our patients, ensuring you feel valued. Our patient-centric clinic offers a convenient and tailored suite of treatments and services designed to meet your individual needs.

Comprehensive Private GP services in Stanmore Broadway, North-West London

Conveniently located in the heart of Stanmore, our modern clinic is registered and regulated by the Care Quality Commission (CQC), ensuring we deliver safe, effective, and high-quality services. All our doctors are registered with the General Medical Council (GMC). We provide flexible appointment options, including face-to-face, telephone, and video consultations, with evening and weekend availability.

Private GP Services & Home Visits

Book a private GP consultation with our GMC-registered doctors in Stanmore. We offer comprehensive healthcare tailored to your needs, with flexible face-to-face, remote and home visit consultations.

Blood Test & Scans

We offer a full range of blood tests and diagnostics for various health conditions, including routine health check blood tests to monitor your overall well-being. Our comprehensive approach ensures early detection and effective management of health concerns.

Health Checks

Health screening is a vital part of preventive healthcare, enabling individuals to assess their overall health and identify potential medical issues early.

Weight Loss Services

Our weight loss clinic offers personalised plans including GLP-1 treatments such as Mounjaro and Wegovy/Ozempic under close medical supervision, alongside nutrition and lifestyle guidance for sustainable weight loss.

Sexual Health

Our sexual health service offers same-day consultations and confidential STI testing, with rapid assessment, expert advice, and appropriate treatment to support your sexual health and wellbeing.

Menopause Clinic

We specialise in women’s health during menopause, with GPs specialising in menopause care. We offer consultations to manage symptoms like hot flushes and mood swings, helping you improve your quality of life.

MSK Physiotherapy

Our skilled Musculoskeletal Physiotherapist offers personalised treatment plans for injury recovery, chronic pain management, improved mobility, and joint or soft tissue injections when appropriate to aid recovery.

Men's Health & TRT

We prioritise men’s health with GP-led assessment and personalised treatment, including testosterone replacement therapy (TRT), with consultations and ongoing monitoring in a confidential, supportive setting.

Psychologist​

Our experienced psychologists offer a safe, supportive space with personalised therapy to help you manage emotional challenges and take control of your mental wellbeing.

Joint Injections​

We offer tailored care for joint and musculoskeletal issues, using treatments such as steroid or hyaluronic acid injections when needed to relieve pain, support recovery, and improve mobility—all while prioritising your safety and comfort.

Dermatology Services​

Our specialist GP offers expert dermatology care for acne, rashes, pigmentation, and mole checks. Focusing on early diagnosis and personalised treatment, we provide safe, timely, and confidential care tailored to your needs.

Minor Surgery & Cryosurgery

Our clinicians offer minor surgical procedures, including removal of skin tags, moles, cysts, and lipomas, as well as CryoPen cryosurgery for benign skin lesions, delivered safely with expert care and aftercare.
