Questions people ask before they book

Do I have to leave my NHS GP?

No.

Niva works alongside your NHS care, not instead of it. You stay registered exactly where you are and use us whenever you want faster access, a longer appointment or a second opinion.

Does Niva Medical Clinic offer same-day appointments?

We generally offer same-day appointments for most patients. If online booking shows no availability, contact us and we will try to accommodate you.

How long is an appointment?

Standard GP consultations are 20 minutes. For complex issues, a 30-minute appointment is recommended.

Do you offer remote appointments?

Yes, we provide telephone and video GP appointments for your convenience. We offer face-to-face appointments at our Stanmore clinic, with nationwide coverage for remote consultations.

Do I have to call to book an appointment, or can I do it online?

You can book online using the ‘Book Online’ button, call or email us, or book at our reception desk.

Can your GP doctors give me a referral letter to see a specialist?

Yes. NHS referrals can be processed with your consent. Private referrals can also be arranged during your appointment.

Do you charge extra for prescriptions or referrals?

No, referrals and private prescriptions are included. Prescription dispensing fees at pharmacies may apply.

Will my insurance cover it?

To confirm whether your insurance covers your appointment, please check with your provider before you book. Some insurers have an annual cash allowance for private GP appointments that can be reimbursed, and the majority of international medical policies cover our services.

Do you offer blood tests at the clinic?

We offer in-house blood testing. For other tests, such as imaging, we refer you to trusted private providers locally.

Are you a walk-in clinic?

We strive to provide same-day appointments, or within 24 to 48 hours. Contact us if it is urgent and we will do our best to accommodate you.

What if I need to change or cancel my appointment?

You can reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours in advance. If you need to cancel, please let us know at least 24 hours before so we can offer the slot to another patient. Cancellations with less than 4 hours’ notice are charged 100% of the appointment fee.
